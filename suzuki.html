About

The Suzuki story

Suzuki makes fast, good-value bikes with plenty of character. The GSX-R sport bikes and the legendary Hayabusa are what the brand is famous for.

Suzuki began as a loom (weaving machine) maker. Motorised bicycles in 1952 led to full motorcycles and a strong racing record. The GSX-R750 of 1985 invented the modern race-replica, and the Hayabusa of 1999 shook the world with its top speed.

Why riders like it

Strengths

Key moments

1909
Suzuki Loom Works

Michio Suzuki builds weaving looms in Hamamatsu.

1952
Power Free

Suzuki’s first motorised bicycle.

1976
Barry Sheene

The British rider wins the 500 cc world title on a Suzuki (again in 1977).

1981
Katana

A sharp, sword-like design that looked like nothing else.

1985
GSX-R750

Race-bred and lightweight: the first modern sport-bike formula.

1999
Hayabusa

About 312 km/h (194 mph): the fastest production bike of its day.

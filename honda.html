About

The Honda story

Honda is known for building motorcycles that are easy to live with: smooth, reliable and well finished. It makes everything from the tiny Super Cub to MotoGP racers.

Soichiro Honda started by fitting small engines to bicycles in Hamamatsu after the war. By the late 1950s the Super Cub made Honda a global name, and in 1969 the CB750 Four showed the world what a modern “superbike” could be. Honda still builds everything from scooters to MotoGP racers.

Why riders like it

Strengths

  • Reliability and build quality that riders trust for years
  • A very wide range: scooters, commuters, sport, touring and adventure
  • A long racing record that feeds into its road bikes

History

Key moments

From the start of Honda to today.

1948
Honda is founded

Honda Motor Co. starts in Hamamatsu, Japan.

1958
Super Cub

A simple, friendly step-through bike; over 100 million have been built.

1959
Isle of Man TT

Honda’s first race abroad – the start of a racing legend.

1969
CB750 Four

Four cylinders, a disc brake and electric start for everyone: the first mass-produced superbike.

1975
Gold Wing

A smooth, water-cooled flat-four touring flagship.

1992
CBR900RR Fireblade

Light weight beats raw size: a new idea for sport bikes.
